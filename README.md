I'm a data science master's student in the **Erasmus Mundus EDISS** programme at Åbo Akademi University in Turku, with a BS in Computer Science (Magna Cum Laude) from FIU. I build AI agents, data pipelines and dashboards, and I care about the part most demos skip: **measuring whether they're right**. The goal is always the same: help organisations make better decisions with data and AI.

- 🏆 **Won the Turku Energia challenge** at Since AI 2026 and reached the **top 5** overall with **Tasehinta**, a price forecast and decision dashboard for electricity traders, **36.7 % better** than the organisers' baseline
- 🛡️ Built **Trust Before Transaction**, my entry to the **Open Agentic Web Challenge**: crash-tests, signed identity and a fair-deal arbiter for AI agents
- 🤖 Built **n8n AI agents** for Wayfair's category team, then rebuilt them as **Market Scout**, a LangGraph agent whose every claim is checked against its source
- 🔬 Researching **LLM agents for energy-aware microservice refactoring** with the University of L'Aquila, and published on **reinforcement learning** for post-disaster road restoration
- 🖤 Founder of **Stray Club**, an alt clothing label from Turku with a store I built from scratch
